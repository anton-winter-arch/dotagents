# CHAT-NOTES-INTERNAL
[YYYY-MONTH](#YYYY-MONTH)

Chat among the delivery team, newest first. Threads with the client go to [[notes-chat-notes-client]], and email goes to [[notes-email-notes]].

Record what a conversation settled, not the conversation. Paraphrase to the point, and quote verbatim only where the exact wording is the thing that matters. A thread that produced nothing durable does not need an entry.

> [!note] Entry format
> One H2 per conversation, `## YYYY-MM-DD-<WHO>`, where `<WHO>` is the participants' initials joined with hyphens, `ALL` for a team channel, or a topic in capitals for a long thread on one subject. Each month opens with a `# YYYY-MONTH` H1 and gets a jump link at the top of the file. Every subsection is optional.

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD-AB-CD

<what was said and what it settled>

### DECISIONS
- YYYY-MM-DD - <what was settled> - supersedes YYYY-MM-DD <ref>, <what changed>

### ACTIONS
- [ ] <action> (owner: <ROLE>; opened YYYY-MM-DD)
```
